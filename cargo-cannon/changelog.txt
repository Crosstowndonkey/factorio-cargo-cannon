Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-04
  Features:
    - Cargo cannon and cargo receiver: fire one stack of cargo per capsule between a planet and the space
      platforms above it (orbit shots), and after research across space to other planets (interplanetary shots).
    - Pick the cargo in the cannon's window: slot 2 only accepts that item (locked until one is picked), so
      inserters and robots can't fill it with capsules.
    - Each shot needs capsules and a full charge of power; costs and flight times are mod settings.
    - A capsule that lands on a full receiver, or where there's no receiver, explodes like a grenade and the cargo
      is lost. Capsules aimed at a platform that has left orbit miss.
    - Circuit control: an enable / disable condition like an inserter's (one signal, Anything or Everything), or
      pick the target by signal (receiver channels). The cannon reports Receiver space and Cannon charge to its
      circuit network, and its contents like a chest.
    - The cannon is a requester chest and the receiver a passive provider for robots; inserters work with both.
    - Settings are copied by copy / paste and kept in blueprints.
    - Optional: with Space Exploration's graphics mods installed (space-exploration-graphics and
      space-exploration-graphics-5), the buildings, capsules and icons use Earendel's delivery cannon art, with his
      permission. A startup setting turns it off. Building sizes stay the same either way.
